## 6. Limitations

- The history tools and the memory tools are built outside the registry
  and take the default budget's page rather than the agent's.
- `preview_chars` reaches `search_files` only as the module default.
- A background command's output file is in the runner's temp, not the
  workspace; `command_output` pages it, `read_file` does not reach it
  deployed (there is no background deployed).
- The provider's metadata for `max_tokens` and the window is roadmap 33's;
  until then the set's numbers stand.
